The South River Leather Co.

Durable Goods & Leather Restoration

While our fine art and hats travel far, The South River Leather Co. is dedicated to the tools and companions of daily life. We make rugged, hand-crafted wallets, custom knife sheaths, field holsters, heavy-duty belts, bags, and stout bracelets designed to take years of honest punishment. Every stitch is placed by hand, and every edge is sealed for the road.

We also undertake leather repairs and restoration—giving worn, family-heirloom bags, torn sheaths, and weathered saddles a second lifetime of faithful service.
