About the Workshop

Every piece under jorjART and The South River Hat Co. is designed, hand-cut, wet-formed, and finished by a single pair of hands inside a mobile workshop.

No assembly lines and no mass production. When you see a hat in person or order a custom commission, you are getting an unrepeatable piece crafted with traditional hand tools, premium vegetable-tanned hides, and time.

  • Studio: Mobile Workshop / Nomadic Lab
  • Primary Focus: Wet-Formed Headwear & Custom Leather
  • Lead Time: 4–6 Weeks on Custom Builds
  • Umbrella: jorjART Studio
