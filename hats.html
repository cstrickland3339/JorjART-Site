Commissioning headwear from South River Hat Co. means owning the story of its creation. You don't just receive a hat in a box—you receive a comprehensive photo history following your project step by step: selecting and cutting the raw veg-tan hide, hand-carving custom brim and hatband details, hand-punching every stitch line, setting each seam, and edge burnishing.

We honor master pattern designers by adapting proven historical blueprints from respected independent artisans, pairing classic headwear geometry with our own hand-carved accents, dyed finishes, and interior leather sweatbands.
